# ddplot 1.0.0

### Breaking changes:
- All arguments now use snake_case, throughout the package. Each capital letter becomes an underscore followed by the lower-case letter (`titleFontSize` is now `title_font_size`, `strokeWidth` is `stroke_width`, `axisCol` is `axis_col`, `colorCategory` is `color_category`, `valueFormat` is `value_format`, `topN` is `top_n`), and lower-case names made of several words are split too: `bgcol` is now `bg_col`, `xtitle`/`ytitle` are `x_title`/`y_title`, `xticks`/`yticks` are `x_ticks`/`y_ticks`, `xtitleFontSize` is `x_title_font_size`, `hlines`/`vlines` are `h_lines`/`v_lines`, `rollmean` is `roll_mean` (`line_chart()`), `meanline` is `mean_line` (`violin_plot()`), and `panelcol`/`xgridlinecol` are `panel_col`/`x_grid_line_col` (`bar_chart_race()`). Code written for ddplot 0.2.0 must be updated to the new names.

### Improvements:
- `bar_chart()` and `horz_bar_chart()` have been rewritten and now share one interactive D3 script. New arguments: `group` with `position = "grouped"`, `"stacked"` or `"percent"` (switchable from buttons on the chart), `agg` (`"sum"`, `"mean"`, `"median"`, `"min"`, `"max"`, `"count"`) so raw rows can be passed directly (leave `y`/`value` empty to count rows), `labels` (values on the bars), `highlight`, `ref_lines`, `negative_fill`, `corner_radius`, `legend`, `controls`, `tooltip`, `animate`, `value_format`/`value_prefix`/`value_suffix`, `subtitle` and `grid_col`. Bars grow in when drawn; the tooltip shows the value, share of total and rank (or every group and the total); buttons re-sort the bars with an animation; the legend hides (click) or isolates (double-click) groups. Negative values are supported. Long category labels are rotated or truncated automatically. In Shiny, `input$<id>_clicked` returns the clicked category and group, and the layout, sort order and hidden groups are kept across `renderD3()` updates. Existing calls keep working; the default look changed (white background, palette colour instead of crimson unless `fill` is set, value labels, rounded bar ends, value-axis title defaults to the column name, smaller fonts), and `x_ticks` in `bar_chart()` is no longer used.
- `lollipop_chart()` and `horz_lollipop()` have been rewritten on the same interactive script as the bar charts. New arguments: `group` (one lollipop per group, side by side, with a click/double-click legend), `agg` (raw rows are aggregated; leave `y`/`value` empty to count rows), `labels`, `highlight`, `ref_lines`, `negative_fill`, `legend`, `controls`, `tooltip`, `animate`, `value_format`/`value_prefix`/`value_suffix`, `subtitle`, `grid_col` and `opacity`. Lollipops grow in when drawn, show their values, can be re-sorted from buttons on the chart, and show their value, rank and (for sums and counts) share of the total on hover. Negative values are drawn downwards (or to the left). In Shiny, `input$<id>_clicked` returns the clicked category and group, and the sort order and hidden groups are kept across `renderD3()` updates. Breaking changes: several rows with the same category are now combined (summed by default, see `agg`) instead of being drawn on top of each other (use `beeswarm_plot()` to show every row); `y`/`value` are now optional; the default look changed (palette colour instead of maroon and lime, `line_stroke_width = 2`, `circle_radius = 6`, a ring in the background colour around the dots, value-axis title defaults to the column name, smaller fonts); `x_ticks` and `label_ticks` are no longer used.
- In `bar_chart()` and `horz_bar_chart()`, the tooltip no longer shows a share of the total or a total when the values are means, medians, minima or maxima (`agg`), and, in the side-by-side layout, categories are then sorted by the average of their groups rather than their total.
- `area_chart()`, `stacked_area_chart()` and `area_band()` have been rewritten on the interactive script of `line_chart()`: they get its crosshair tooltip, range buttons, navigator, drag-to-zoom (double-click to reset), click/double-click legend, end labels, `events`, `bands`, `h_lines`, value and date formats, `subtitle`, and `ts`/`mts` input (for `area_chart()` and `stacked_area_chart()`). `area_chart()` gains several series (`y = c(...)` or `group`), `baseline` and `negative_fill` (area above and below a value in two colours) and `curve`. `stacked_area_chart()` gains Stacked / 100% buttons (`position`, `controls`), a tooltip with every layer, its share and the total, layers that re-stack when one is hidden, long data (`y` + `group`), `y` to choose the columns and `colors`; missing values count as zero when stacking and negative values are stacked below zero. `area_band()` gains an optional central line (`y`, `line_width`), one band per `group`, and a tooltip with the range. In Shiny, the zoom, the hidden series and the stacked layout are kept across `renderD3()` updates. Existing calls keep working, but the defaults changed: white background, palette colours instead of crimson (and of the D3 Category10 scheme in `stacked_area_chart()`; `color_category` still picks a scheme), smaller fonts, a gradient fill in `area_chart()` unless `opacity` is given, `stroke_width = 1` and `opacity = 0.85` in `stacked_area_chart()`, a semi-transparent band in `area_band()`. `stacked_area_chart()` now stacks the numeric columns only, and `legend_box_size`/`legend_text_size` are no longer used.
- `histogram()` has been rewritten as an interactive chart. New arguments: `data` (give a data frame and column names), `group` with `position = "stacked"` or `"overlay"`, `stat = "count"`, `"percent"` or `"density"`, `bin_width` and `breaks`, `density` (kernel density or normal curve), `stats` (mean and median lines), `ref_lines`, `rug`, `summary`, `x_format`/`x_prefix`/`x_suffix`, `legend`, `controls`, `tooltip`, `animate`, `corner_radius`, `subtitle` and `grid_col`. The number of bins, counts / percentages and the group layout can be changed from buttons on the chart; hovering a bin shows its range, count, share and cumulative share; a summary (n, mean, median, standard deviation) is shown above the bars; dragging across the bars selects a range, which can be zoomed into and re-binned (double-click resets); the legend hides (click) or isolates (double-click) groups. Integer data get bins centred on the integers. In Shiny, `input$<id>_clicked` and `input$<id>_selected` return the clicked bin and the selected range, and the bins, layout, hidden groups and zoom are kept across `renderD3()` updates. Existing calls keep working; the default look changed (white background, palette colour instead of crimson unless `fill` is set, bins with round widths so `bins` is approximate, x-axis title defaults to the variable name, smaller fonts).
- `pie_chart()` has been rewritten as an interactive chart. `value` is now optional (rows are counted) and rows with the same label are added up. New arguments: `fill`, `sort`, `max_slices`/`other_label` (combine the smallest slices), `labels` and `label_position` (labels inside the slices, or outside with leader lines), `legend`, `center_label` (donut centre), `highlight`, `start_angle`/`end_angle` (e.g. half donuts), `stroke`/`stroke_width`, `tooltip`, `animate`, `value_format`/`value_prefix`/`value_suffix`, `subtitle` and `axis_col`. Slices sweep in, pull out on hover with a tooltip (value, share, rank; the "Other" slice lists what it contains), and can be hidden (click) or isolated (double-click) from the legend, the shares being recomputed; donut charts show the total, or the hovered slice, in their centre. `inner_radius` below 1 is now a fraction of the radius. In Shiny, `input$<id>_clicked` returns the clicked label and the hidden slices are kept across `renderD3()` updates. Existing calls keep working; the default look changed (palette colours instead of the "Paired" scheme unless `color_category` is set, lines between the slices, percentages on the slices, a legend with the shares on the right or below, the title at the top left, `label_height` defaults to 12, `pad_radius` now defaults to the D3 default so `pad_angle` works on its own).
- `parliament_chart()` has been rewritten as an interactive hemicycle and coalition builder. The seats are laid out on as many rows as give the largest seats, and the parties form wedges from left to right; a dashed line marks the majority and the centre shows the total. Hovering a seat or a legend entry shows the party's seats, share, rank and distance from a majority; clicking parties builds a coalition whose seats glide to the left, with a gauge counting them against the majority. New arguments: `subtitle`, `fill`, `color_category`, `previous` (gains and losses since the previous election), `majority` (a number or share of seats, or `FALSE`), `coalition`, `coalition_builder`, `regroup`, `sort`, `arc` (from a hemicycle to a full ring), `inner_radius`, `seat_shape`, `center_label`, `legend`, `tooltip`, `animate` and `axis_col`. The seats fill in from left to right when the chart comes into view. In Shiny, `input$<id>_clicked` returns the clicked party and `input$<id>_coalition` the coalition, which is kept across `renderD3()` updates while the seats glide to their new places. Bug fixes: the chart now works with any column names (it only drew seats for columns named `political_party` and `number_of_seats`), `width` and `height` are applied, seats that did not fit are no longer dropped, and the tooltip no longer leaks into the page. Existing calls keep working; the default look changed (white background, palette colours, seats sized to fill the chart unless `seat_size` is given, title at the top left, `title_font_size = 20`).
- `scatter_plot()` has been upgraded. New arguments: `group` (categorical colours with a click/double-click legend, or a colour gradient for a numeric column), `size_by`/`size_range` (bubble chart), `label`/`show_labels` (tooltip titles and collision-free point labels), `tooltip` (extra columns), `trend = "lm"`/`"loess"` with a 95% confidence band (`ci`) and fit statistics on hover, `x_scale`/`y_scale = "log"`, `h_lines`/`v_lines`, `x_format`/`y_format`, `subtitle` and `grid_col`. Hovering a point shows its values with guides to both axes; dragging a box selects points, which can be zoomed into (double-click resets). In Shiny, `input$<id>_selected` and `input$<id>_clicked` return the selected and clicked row numbers, and hidden groups and the zoom are kept across `renderD3()` updates. Existing calls keep working; the default look changed (white background, grid lines, palette colour instead of crimson unless `col` is set, `size = 4`, `opacity = 0.8`, axis titles default to the column names, smaller title font sizes).

### Race charts:
- A new family of animated race charts shares one player: a play / pause button, a timeline that can be dragged to any moment, a speed button (0.5x to 4x), a loop button and keyboard controls (space, arrow keys, Home, End). The races animate continuously between periods, which can be numbers, dates or text; items can enter late, leave early or have gaps; tooltips keep updating while the race plays; the race starts when the chart scrolls into view (`autoplay`, `start_frame`, `loop`, `controls`, `frame_dur`, `ease`, `time_format`). In Shiny, `input$<id>_frame` reports the period on show and the period, speed and pinned or hidden items are kept across `renderD3()` updates. A new "Race charts" guide shows them all.
- `bar_chart_race()` has been rewritten on this player: bars overtake each other exactly when their values cross, new items rise from below the cut and leaving ones sink, value labels count smoothly, a big period label (with an optional `total` counter) sits in the corner, hovering shows the value, rank and change since the previous period, and clicking a bar follows it, even below the cut. New arguments: `top_n` (with Top N buttons), `group` (colours and a clickable legend), `fill`, `highlight`, `labels`, `name_position`, `total`, `rank_flash`, `agg`, `corner_radius`, `subtitle`, value formats, `tooltip`, `axis_col` and the player arguments. Existing calls keep working; the default look changed (white background, palette colours, no stroke or panel), and `frame_dur` / `transition_dur` now default to 0 / 1000 (each period lasts `frame_dur + transition_dur`). `sort = "none"` now works.
- New `line_race()`: a racing line chart where lines grow over time with live labels at their heads, smoothly rescaling axes, a value / rank (bump chart) switch, `top_n` leaders over grey context lines, a sliding `window` or growing time axis, pop-up `events`, crosshair tooltips, pinning and a category legend.
- New `bubble_race()`: an animated Gapminder style bubble chart with fixed linear or log axes (switchable from the chart), a period watermark, live tooltips with axis guides, click to follow bubbles with their trails (hover or click a trail dot to jump in time), a group legend, a size legend and Shiny inputs (`_clicked`, `_followed`).
- New `radial_bar_race()`: a circular bar chart race with spokes and rings layouts that morph into each other, smoothly moving ranks, a live centre counter, rank-change arrows, live tooltips, click-to-pin and a group legend.
- New `treemap_race()`: an animated treemap whose tiles glide as the values change (stable re-squarified layout), with optional groups (headers, zoom on click, legend to hide or isolate), live tooltips, pinned items, value / share / growth labels and a growth heat map colour mode.
- New `donut_race()`: an animated share race, a donut or pie whose slices glide past each other when the ranking changes, with a live league table (share bars, values, rank change arrows), an "Other" slice that items flow into and out of (`max_slices`), hover and pin, hide and show all, and a ranked or fixed slice order.

### Playful indicators:
- New `nose_growth()`: a person in profile whose nose grows with a level, from a small nose (0) to one that reaches across the chart (1), with stages from "Honest" to "Pants on fire". A twig with leaves sprouts from a long nose and a bird lands on the longest (`leaves`, `bird`); the person gets more nervous (wavy mouth, raised brow, blush, a drop of sweat) as the nose grows. The eye follows the mouse, the ponytail swings, and clicking makes the nose wobble, the bird hop and the person protest in a speech bubble. It has `target`, `draggable` (the tip follows the mouse), colours for the skin, hair, stripes and card, and the Shiny inputs of the other indicators.
- New `hair_growth()`: a portrait whose hair grows with a level, from a bald, shiny head (0) through stubble, a buzz cut, short curls and curly hair to a big afro with an afro pick (1). The face smiles more as the hair grows, the eyes follow the mouse, the person blinks and breathes, and clicking makes the afro bounce and the person wink. It has `target` (a dashed outline of the hair to reach), `draggable` (drag up to grow, down to cut), colours for the hair, skin, turtleneck and card, and the Shiny inputs of the other indicators (`input$<id>_clicked` with the stage, `input$<id>_value`).
- `heart_fill()`, `glass_fill()`, `plant_growth()`, `flame()` and `flower()` have been rewritten as animated, interactive indicators sharing one script of helpers. They animate when they scroll into view (the heart and the glass fill up, the plant grows, the flame ignites, the flower blooms) while the value counts up, only animate while on screen, stay still for visitors who prefer reduced motion, and fit the size of the widget (`width`, `height` and `bg_col` are now available everywhere). In Shiny, a new value moves smoothly from the old one, `input$<id>_clicked` reports clicks and, with the new `draggable = TRUE` (heart, glass, plant and flame), viewers can set the level by dragging or with the arrow keys, reported in `input$<id>_value`. Existing calls keep working; the size arguments (`heart_size`, `glass_width`, `glass_height`, `pot_width`, `pot_height`, `plant_max_height`, `petal_length`, `petal_width`, `center_radius`) now default to fitting the chart, font sizes can be numbers or CSS sizes, and the default colours changed.
- `heart_fill()`: a classic heart shape with a wavy liquid, a gloss, a heartbeat (`beat`, `bpm`), the percentage inside the heart (white over the liquid) and a `target` line; hover to speed up the beat, click to release small hearts.
- `glass_fill()`: a tapered glass with reflections, a moving surface (`waves`), rising `bubbles`, an optional measuring scale (`ticks`) and `target`; moving the mouse rocks the water, clicking drops some more in.
- `plant_growth()`: the plant grows from a seed to a sprout, leaves, a bud and an opening flower, sways in the breeze (`sway`), shows its stage and an optional `target`; hover for a gust of wind, click to water it. New `label_color`, `label_font_size` and `soil_color`; `flower_color` is now used.
- `flame()`: a three-layer flickering flame with a glow and sparks (`flicker`, `sparks`), on its own, a candle or logs (`base`), its height scaled to `max_intensity`, an optional value and caption (`show_value`, `value_suffix`, `title_text`); it leans away from the mouse and flares up when clicked. `flame_outline` now defaults to no outline, and `width` and `height` are now applied.
- `flower()`: gradient petals in two rings (`layers`), stamens on a golden-angle spiral (`stamens`), a bloom when it comes into view (`bloom`), and petals to pluck ("loves me, loves me not", `pluck`). With `petal_values` and `petal_labels`, it becomes a small flower chart with tooltips and highlighting. It stops turning while hovered, and `rotation_speed` now defaults to 0.5.

# ddplot 0.2.0

### Improvements:
- `line_chart()` has been rewritten for time series work. It now supports several series (wide `y = c(...)`, long `group =`, or `ts`/`mts` objects passed directly), a crosshair tooltip showing every series at the hovered date, a click/double-click legend, direct end labels, range buttons (1M ... All), a navigator brush, drag-to-zoom with an auto-rescaling y axis, `transform = "index"`/`"percent"` rebasing, `rollmean`, `lower`/`upper` forecast ribbons, `bands`, `events`, `hlines`, `yScale = "log"`, `area` and `points`. In Shiny, the zoom range and hidden series are kept across `renderD3()` updates. Existing calls keep working; the default look changed (white background, `strokeWidth = 2`, `titleFontSize = 20`, `xtitleFontSize`/`ytitleFontSize = 13`, left-aligned title, y-axis title above the axis, palette color instead of crimson for a single series unless `stroke` is set, y axis fitted to the data unless `yZero = TRUE` or `area = TRUE`).

### Documentation:
- The website has been redesigned (Bootstrap 5, light and dark themes). It has a new home page, a filterable gallery of every chart, and one guide per family of charts (time series, comparisons, distributions, relationships, part-to-whole, KPIs, playful indicators), ordered from the most commonly needed charts to the more specialised ones. The reference index is grouped the same way. The "Get started" vignette is now a short introduction; the chart-by-chart examples moved to the website guides.

### Bug fixes:
- Charts now redraw correctly in `Shiny` with `r2d3::d3Output()` / `r2d3::renderD3()`. Previously r2d3 re-ran the chart script on the same SVG, so updates were drawn on top of the previous chart (or not visibly at all) and animation timers kept running. A shared preamble (`ddplotreset.js`) now clears the previous render and stops its timers before each redraw; this also fixes stacked timers after a widget resize.

### Adding new functions:
- `slope_chart`: Tufte / New York Times style slopegraph comparing two values per category, with direction colouring, `highlight`, label collision avoidance and hover-to-focus.
- `bump_chart`: ranking-over-time chart (Financial Times, The Economist, FiveThirtyEight) with ranks computed per period (or supplied via `ranked = TRUE`), rank circles, end labels and `highlight`.
- `ridgeline_plot`: "joy plot" of stacked kernel densities per group, with Gaussian/Epanechnikov kernels, `overlap`, `sort`, mean/median markers and hover tooltips.
- `treemap_chart`: Shneiderman / NYT budget style treemap with optional `group` hierarchy, several tiling algorithms, auto-fitting labels, group headers and an in-SVG tooltip.
- `waffle_chart`: Pew / FiveThirtyEight style square-grid part-to-whole chart using largest-remainder allocation, row or column fill, animated entry and hover highlighting.
- `sunburst_chart`: zoomable sunburst for hierarchical data (inspired by Apache ECharts), with click-to-zoom, branch highlighting on hover and smooth arc transitions.
- `radar_chart`: radar / spider chart (inspired by Chart.js) with polygon or circular grid, shared or per-axis scaling, center-out animation, point tooltips and a click-to-toggle legend.
- `calendar_heatmap`: GitHub-style calendar heatmap (inspired by Frappe Charts) with one panel per year, Sunday/Monday week start, a hoverable "Less ... More" legend and a staggered fade-in.
- `chord_diagram`: chord diagram (inspired by Nivo) from an edge list or square matrix, with hover-to-fade, flow tooltips, gradient ribbons and click-to-pin.
- `streamgraph`: streamgraph (inspired by Highcharts) with wiggle/silhouette/expand baselines, inline layer labels, crosshair tooltip and a click-to-toggle legend.
- `violin_plot`: violin plot (inspired by Plotly.js) with per-group Gaussian KDE, inner box plot, mean line, jittered points and a summary-statistics tooltip.
- `mosaic_chart`: mosaic / Marimekko chart (inspired by Vega-Lite) with variable-width columns, 100%-stacked segments, in-cell labels and share tooltips.
- `radial_bar_chart`: concentric circular progress rings (inspired by ApexCharts' radialBar) with rounded arcs, sweep-in animation and a center readout that updates on hover.
- `rose_chart`: Nightingale rose / polar area chart (inspired by AntV G2) with area-true square-root radii, optional stacking, pop-out hover and a clickable legend.
- `hexbin_plot`: hexagonal binning plot (inspired by Observable Plot's hexbin) coloured by count on a linear, sqrt or log scale, with optional size-by-count and a continuous legend.
